JMA example catalogue, as distributed with Zhuang's stochastic declustering software
(https://bemlar.ism.ac.jp/zhuang/software.html).

alljpM45.etas  Japan Meteorological Agency catalogue, M >= 4.5, 1926-2007 (13,724 events).
               Header line, then: id lon lat mag t(days since the first event, 1926-01-08) depth year month day

Reference: Zhuang, J., Ogata, Y. & Vere-Jones, D. (2002). Stochastic declustering of space-time
earthquake occurrences. J. Am. Stat. Assoc. 97, 369-380. Data: Japan Meteorological Agency.
